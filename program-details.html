1st to 5th Grade

Primary Education

The primary years are crucial for building a strong academic and emotional foundation. Our primary program focuses on core subjects while keeping learning interactive and joyful.

  • ✓ Core Subjects: Mathematics, Science, English, and Environmental Studies.
  • ✓ Interactive Learning: Storytelling, visual aids, and interactive games.
  • ✓ Personalized Attention: Small batch sizes to ensure every child gets noticed.
  • ✓ Timings: 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM

6th to 8th Grade

Middle School

As students transition into middle school, we introduce advanced concepts and foster independent thinking. This is where curiosity meets structured academic rigor.

  • ✓ Advanced Curriculum: Deep dive into Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and complex Mathematics.
  • ✓ AI Modules: Introduction to modern AI tools and basic logic building to stay ahead of the curve.
  • ✓ Concept Clarity: Focus on 'why' and 'how' rather than rote memorization.
  • ✓ Timings: 4:30 PM - 7:00 PM
Enroll in Middle School
9th & 10th Grade

High School (Board Prep)

The high school program is an intensive coaching regimen designed to prepare students for their board exams with confidence and excellence.

  • ✓ Intensive Coaching: Comprehensive coverage of the entire board syllabus well ahead of time.
  • ✓ Mock Exams: Regular test series, previous year paper solving, and time management strategies.
  • ✓ Practical Labs: Hands-on experiments to solidify theoretical science concepts.
  • ✓ Timings: 5:00 PM - 8:30 PM
